this was taken on a bridge over the suzhou river, one of the main tributaries to the huangpu. you can see what might be one of the first post offices in shanghai in the distance (the building w/ the clock tower). there's also a bellagio hotel on the far right of the pic. according to my grandpa it used to be shanghai's first hospital!
this is the view from the other side of the bridge. apparently the suzhou river used to be clean and green before rapid industrial development turned it basically black. the government then stepped in (probably before i was born) to stop cargo ships from coming through and the river reverted back to the green you see in this photo
also the red and white building i circled in red used to be the ussr embassy. now it's converted to working for russia (obviously)
this used to be a movie theater! i actually don't remember if he said it was still operating haha. but back then ticket prices were 5 fen (rmb equivalent of cents)!
