anime expo pt 2

man, time sure flies. i swear i blink and another anime expo has come and gone.

initially i thought this one was going to be hella scuffed, since i was working on the other side of the goddamn country for the summer and had to accommodate for 6 hour flights both ways. everything actually turned out pretty alright though, since work turned out to be hybrid.

i wound up booking the flight to la one day before the actual event and the flight back one day after to save some money, and convinced my angel of a friend to let me crash at their place for the two nights we didn't have the airbnb booked.

also, this time i was going with four others: idkac and conservemomenta from last year, as well as two other friends i'll just call canary and rouge. as such, the uber prices were basically halved for me. i think in the end i didn't even spend over a thousand at the actual event.

like with the other blog post, all the arknights stuff is at the bottom
i think i took more photos than last time, so i split it into pics i took during the meetup and random pics i took all throughout the entire con

oh yeah, uh, one more thing. i realize as i'm looking through all these photos now that a lot of them have AWFUL quality, glare, lighting, whatever. like even given my poor camera skills some of these are kinda bad
eh whatever, i guess i'll just have to remember to clean my camera more next time
or maybe if i lock in i'll bring an actual camera?
